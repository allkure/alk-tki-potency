# Methods

## Dataset compilation

We assembled a harmonized dataset of ALK tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) potency measurements against ALK kinase-domain variants by manually curating primary and supplementary materials from seven peer-reviewed publications spanning 2016–2024 (Table 1). Sources were selected to maximize coverage of clinically-approved ALK-TKIs (crizotinib, ceritinib, alectinib, brigatinib, lorlatinib) and next-generation compounds (zotizalkib, neladalkib, repotrectinib, ensartinib, entrectinib, gilteritinib) against the ALK-resistance mutation spectrum reported in NSCLC, prioritizing studies that systematically profiled multiple variants against multiple drugs under matched experimental conditions within each paper. Numerical half-maximal inhibitory concentration (IC50) values were extracted from published tables, figure legends, and supplementary files; dose-response curves without accompanying printed values were excluded. Off-target selectivity anchors against ROS1 and TRKA/B/C were retained where reported alongside ALK measurements. Records where the drug did not reach IC50 at the highest concentration tested were retained as right-censored observations and flagged accordingly.

## Data harmonization

All measurements were mapped to a common 45-field schema capturing (i) provenance (paper identifier, DOI, data location, source type); (ii) variant annotation (gene, protein-level variant string, reference/position/alternate amino acid, canonical-numbering compliance flag); (iii) drug identity (canonical name, name as reported); (iv) measurement (value type, reported value with unit, normalized nM concentration, fold-change versus wild-type, upper-bound flag for censored values); (v) statistical descriptors (error type, error magnitude, number of independent experiments); and (vi) assay context (assay class, platform subtype, enzyme construct, ATP concentration and its relation to K_m, substrate identity and concentration, incubation temperature, cell line, expression construct including EML4-ALK fusion isoform, and serum conditions). Two free-text fields — `extractor_notes` and `ambiguity_flag` — recorded per-record uncertainties including inferred versus explicitly-stated EML4-ALK isoform assignment, ENU-mutagenesis clonal versus site-directed reconstructed cell lines, non-EML4 fusion partners (NPM-ALK, SQSTM1-ALK), and non-canonical residue numbering. Compound mutations were represented as `+`-delimited variant strings; where a source did not report a matched within-batch wild-type reference, fold-change was recorded in a separate `fold_change_vs_wt_cross_site` field to preserve the distinction between paper-native and cross-source-computed values. Every extracted record was cross-checked against its source figure or table, and per-paper subsets were validated for internal consistency (wild-type reference values, replicate counts, unit conversions) prior to merging.

## Dataset composition and cross-source concordance

The final dataset comprises **1,417 records** across seven independent laboratories, covering **58 unique ALK variants** (30 single missense substitutions, 2 in-frame indels [G1202del, T1151insT], 24 double compound mutations, and 2 triple compound mutations) and 11 ALK-directed compounds. Of these, 1,233 records are cellular measurements (903 cellular IC50 and 330 cellular E_max values, predominantly Ba/F3 EML4-ALK variant 1 CellTiter-Glo viability at 72 h) and 184 are biochemical kinase-inhibition IC50 values; 45 off-target selectivity records against ROS1 and NTRK1/2/3 kinases are included in the 1,417 (33 biochemical IC50, 12 cellular IC50). Within the harmonized Ba/F3 EML4-ALK v1 cellular subset, and excluding the censored records whose IC50 is reported only as a bound, 361 unique variant × drug combinations were profiled across sources, of which 124 (34%) were independently measured in ≥ 2 laboratories, 62 in ≥ 3, and 28 in ≥ 4, enabling systematic cross-source concordance analysis. Pairwise agreement across all matched cross-laboratory measurements (n = 409 pairs) reached Pearson r = 0.80 and Spearman ρ = 0.81 on log₁₀-transformed IC50; residual laboratory-specific systematic log-offsets, attributable to documented assay-configuration effects — most notably Cheng–Prusoff-predicted apparent-affinity shifts at differing biochemical ATP concentrations and cell-density-dependent viability endpoint sensitivity — are consistent with the physical basis of the assays and are recorded per-record to allow explicit modelling as paper-level effects.
